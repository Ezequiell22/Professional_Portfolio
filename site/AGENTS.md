## Rules

* This is a **professional portfolio and body of work**, NOT a job-seeking website.
* **Never introduce employment, recruitment, hiring, job-search or "opportunities" messaging** unless explicitly requested.
* Do not infer career goals that were not explicitly provided.
* Do not turn the website into a CV or recruitment landing page.
* Focus on **products, case studies, product thinking, AI architecture and engineering**.
* Do not invent metrics, customers, users, revenue, results or professional claims.
* Preserve existing content and structure unless the user explicitly asks to change them.
* Follow the user's **latest request** and keep changes within its scope.
* Do not redesign unrelated parts of the website.
* Prefer **minimal, precise changes** over unnecessary rewrites.
