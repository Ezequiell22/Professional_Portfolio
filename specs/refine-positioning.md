# AI Product Management Portfolio — Positioning Refinement

Refine the existing portfolio implementation. Do NOT rebuild from scratch. Preserve
visual quality, multilingual architecture, responsiveness and existing components
where useful.

The main issue to solve is **positioning**: the portfolio demonstrates AI,
architecture and engineering well, but must communicate more strongly that the
profile is an **AI Product Manager / AI Product professional** — someone who
defines, designs, measures and evolves AI products, not just someone who builds
AI systems.

## 1. New positioning

Primary identity: **AI Product Manager**.

Supporting dimensions: Product Strategy · AI Product Design · AI Solutions
Architecture · AI Engineering.

Central message: **I turn complex business problems into measurable AI products.**

## 2. Stronger Product Management language (concise, not a giant list)

Demonstrate knowledge of Product Strategy, Product Execution, Product Analytics
and AI-specific product metrics (answer accuracy, retrieval quality, hallucination
rate, task completion rate, agent success rate, human intervention rate, latency,
cost per interaction, token consumption, adoption, time saved, automation rate).

Visualize the thinking as: **Business Outcome → Product Metric → AI Metric → Technical Metric**.

## 3. "AI Product Management" section (after Hero)

Lifecycle: PROBLEM → DISCOVERY → OPPORTUNITY → PRODUCT VISION → MVP → AI
EXPERIENCE → ARCHITECTURE → DELIVERY → MEASURE → ITERATE.

Statement: "I don't treat AI as a feature to be added to an existing product…"

## 4. OKRs — "How I measure AI products" (illustrative, not claims)

Objective → Key Results → Product Metrics → AI Metrics → Technical Metrics.

## 5–11. Transform "Selected Work" into "Selected Product Cases"

Structure per case: Problem → Solution → Before → After → Impact → AI/Architecture.
Product first, technology second. One case each for AI Software Factory,
SpecKitBasic, MARK1, Luna and Enterprise MCP Platform.

## 12. No invented quantitative results

Use qualitative impact and "Metrics to measure" instead of fake numbers.

## 13. "How I measure success" — metric chain

BUSINESS OUTCOME → PRODUCT KPI → AI QUALITY → TECHNICAL PERFORMANCE → COST,
with a concrete example.

## 14. "How I build AI products" — 11 product-oriented steps

Understand → Discover → Define → Prioritize → Design → Architect → Build →
Evaluate → Ship → Measure → Iterate.

## 15. Product Prioritization framework

VALUE × CUSTOMER IMPACT × STRATEGIC FIT ÷ EFFORT + RISK.

## 16. Capabilities — Product Management dominant

Product / AI Product / Architecture / Engineering.

## 17. All selected projects labeled "Completed"

Do not use Concept / In Development / Ongoing / Prototype. Do not fabricate
production usage, customers, users or business results.

## 18. Narrative

Business problems → Product Manager thinking → where AI creates value →
measurable outcomes → AI product design → solution architecture → engineering →
measure and iterate.

Desired perception: **AI Product Manager with unusual technical depth.**

## 19. Keep it concise

Do not make the website longer. Improve through hierarchy, product language,
concise case studies, before/after storytelling, metrics, OKRs, outcomes,
prioritization and product decisions.

## 20. Final quality test

Product Leader recognizes PM depth; AI Engineering Leader recognizes technical
depth; Recruiter/Executive understands positioning in 20 seconds.

Final statement: **I am an AI Product Manager who understands how to turn
business problems into measurable AI products — and I have the technical depth
to design and build the systems behind them.**

No job-search language anywhere on the page.
